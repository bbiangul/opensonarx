## What changed

“Spam,” “misinformation,” and “low quality” are not deterministic settlement predicates. A token-holder vote over those labels creates a bribable, gameable oracle. Human or model review may add warnings and evidence, but cannot slash, seize, or redistribute a bond.

## Eligible challenge flow

1. The original commitment pins an exact predicate version, inputs, outcome source, deadline, and refund behavior.
2. A challenger identifies the exact predicate/input alleged to fail and posts a challenge bond.
3. Independent executors reproduce the deterministic trace. Trace equality—not majority authority—drives the declared result.
4. Matching traces execute the pre-declared refund/payout/slash rule.
5. Divergent traces, missing artifacts, or ambiguous outcomes pause the class and return the commitment and challenge bonds as unresolved.

This applies to narrow facts such as signature validity, exact hash/snapshot equality, or a pinned machine-observable event. It does not adjudicate broad truth, journalism, politics, interpretation, or scientific controversy.

## Legacy commands

Commands such as the following may still exist while migration proceeds:

```bash
oi dispute open <target> --bond 10 --reason <reason>
oi jury register
oi jury vote <dispute_id> --verdict uphold
```

They must be treated as legacy/non-binding until a versioned deterministic execution path and full coalition-safety audit are implemented. They are not evidence that subjective slashing is part of v2.4.
