## What are entities?

Entities represent specific creators or accounts on user-generated content (UGC) platforms. They're extracted automatically from URLs during crawling.

| Platform | Entity format | Example |
|----------|--------------|---------|
| YouTube | `youtube.com/c/{channel}` or `youtube.com/@{handle}` | `youtube.com/c/3blue1brown` |
| X (Twitter) | `x.com/{handle}` | `x.com/elonmusk` |
| Reddit | `reddit.com/r/{subreddit}` | `reddit.com/r/rust` |

## Why entity scope still matters

On UGC platforms, a domain represents millions of unrelated creators. Entity identifiers let clients filter results and let future typed source histories refer to the relevant creator rather than incorrectly transferring a platform-wide record.

## Normalization

Entities are automatically normalized today:

- `twitter.com/elonmusk` → `x.com/elonmusk`
- `youtube.com/c/3Blue1Brown` → `youtube.com/c/3blue1brown`
- Case-insensitive, alias-aware

## Available today: Filtering by entity

You can already filter search results by entity:

```bash
oi search "linear algebra" --entity youtube.com/c/3blue1brown
```

Legacy entity stake can be returned as metadata, but cannot boost ranking. A future claim-specific position by an entity must independently satisfy the OpenUncertainty timing, loss, scope, settlement, and held-out calibration gates.
