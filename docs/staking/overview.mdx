## Why stake?

Staking $TRUTH tokens on a domain will be a signal that you believe in the content quality. Staked domains will get a ranking boost in search results. Bad actors who stake on spam will be slashed through the dispute process.

## Planned economics

```
final_score = relevance_score * stake_multiplier * reputation_decay
```

| Factor | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **More stake** | Higher ranking boost (log-scaled) |
| **Positive feedback** | Reputation grows, sustains the boost |
| **Negative feedback** | Reputation decays, reduces the boost |
| **Dispute (upheld)** | Stake slashed entirely |

## Planned stake types

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Domain** | Stake on a publisher's domain | `rust-lang.org` |
| **Entity** | Stake on a specific creator on a UGC platform | `youtube.com/c/3blue1brown` |
| **Curation** | Stake on someone else's domain | `oi domain curate docs.rs --amount 50` |

## UGC platform handling

For user-generated content platforms (YouTube, X, Reddit), **domain-level stake will NOT pass through** to individual pages. You'll need to stake on the specific entity (channel, account, subreddit) for content to get a boost.

This prevents a YouTube domain stake from boosting all 500M+ videos.

## What works today

Today, ranking is based on:
- **Semantic similarity** (HNSW, 384-dim MiniLM embeddings)
- **Keyword matching** (BM25, TF-IDF)
- **Reputation** (Wilson score from feedback signals)

Staking will add a fourth dimension to ranking when Phase 3 launches.
