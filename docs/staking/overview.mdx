## What changed from v1

The planned domain-level stake boost is retracted. Domain stake, wallet balance, reputation, and DNS verification do not enter Stage-1 ranking. Registry data may still appear as legacy accountability metadata while clients migrate, but it cannot purchase relevance.

The remaining useful intuition is narrower: accepting real loss on one exact claim before its outcome can reveal conviction. Whether that conviction predicts the declared target is an empirical question, not a protocol assumption.

## Eligible economic conviction

A position is eligible only if it:

- identifies the exact claim, direction, task, horizon, and deterministic/event-bound settlement rule;
- is committed before material outcome information;
- carries non-zero maximum loss and lock time;
- records how much of the claim the predicate covers; and
- discounts refunds, rebates, self-funding, insurance, side payments, and hedges.

Fully hedged, refundable, post-outcome, unscoped, or subjectively settled positions receive zero economic exposure.

## How it may affect uncertainty

Headline amount is log-normalized and capped, then multiplied by maximum-loss, time, predicate-coverage, funding-independence, and unhedged fractions. Comparable resolved claims learn a likelihood relative to a no-cash baseline.

The learned result may be positive, zero, or negative. Sparse calibration means zero effect. MVP-1 caps the absolute odds effect at 3×, so even a nominal whale position cannot erase strong contrary history.

## What works today

- stake/reputation/DNS-neutral scorer tests;
- a focused default-query registry/no-registry regression;
- typed effective-exposure and calibration code;
- positive, zero, negative, sparse, OOD, and whale unit tests;
- a reproducible synthetic forward benchmark; and
- a leakage-checked real-cohort JSONL evaluator with cluster-bootstrap uncertainty.

See the [OpenUncertainty MVP-1 protocol](../OPENUNCERTAINTY-MVP1.md). Real cash must not affect a live decision policy until a pre-registered cohort passes the calibration, manipulation, wealth-slice, legal, and custody gates.
