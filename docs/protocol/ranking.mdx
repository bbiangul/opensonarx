## Stage 1: broad retrieval

The shipped scorer keeps financial and identity signals out of relevance:

```
base_relevance = semantic_weight * normalized_semantic_score
               + bm25_weight * normalized_bm25_score

final_score = base_relevance * agent_utility_boost
              * quality_multiplier * freshness_multiplier
```

Semantic similarity provides conceptual matching; BM25 preserves exact keyword matching for names, identifiers, functions, and error codes. The quality multiplier uses bounded content-local spam/slop/quality features, while freshness is a bounded time-decay feature.

Stake amount, reputation, DNS verification, wallet balance, payment volume, popularity, and OpenUncertainty outputs do not enter this score or candidate generation. Unit tests cover scorer neutrality, and a focused `HybridIndex` regression returns identical default-query order and scores with and without non-zero registry data.

## Stage 2: claim-level reliance

OpenUncertainty is an experimental decision layer over the retrieved candidate set. For a named claim, task, horizon, and policy it emits one of:

- `DETERMINISTIC`: scoped result from a reproducible predicate;
- `CALIBRATED`: probability plus interval for an observable target with adequate held-out data; or
- `EVIDENTIAL_ONLY`: support, conflict, and ignorance without an invented probability.

A client may use this output to rerank, warn, cite, or abstain. Responses must preserve both `retrieval_rank` and `decision_rank`; Stage 2 cannot silently become a universal reputation order.

## Cash at risk

Raw domain stake never buys rank or confidence. A separate claim-specific position may become an **economic-conviction feature** only when it:

- was posted before the outcome;
- carries genuine, non-refundable, unhedged loss;
- names an exact deterministic/event-bound predicate, task, and horizon; and
- improves held-out calibration for comparable claims.

The learned contribution may be positive, zero, or negative and is capped. If calibration is sparse, its effect is exactly zero. This is evidence of revealed conviction, not purchased truth.

See the [OpenUncertainty MVP-1 protocol](../OPENUNCERTAINTY-MVP1.md) for the executable synthetic ablation, forward JSONL evaluator, attack guards, and real-pilot gates.

<Warning>
  `trust_verdict` and `--min-trust` are legacy compatibility fields derived from old stake/reputation records. The filter is explicit and post-ranking, but it is not a calibrated probability and is not the v2.4 reliance model.
</Warning>
